# Design contract

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md)

## Design contract: iPhone fidelity inside Android

Treat the Island as one display and interaction system, with a hardware anchor, state model, and transition renderer. Match the chosen iPhone's **observed** pixels and gestures while allowing the Android system to keep its own status icons, privacy indicators, notification shade, keyguard, and accessibility working. Log both reference fidelity and Android coexistence for every state; one cannot stand in for the other.

| Layer | Design rule | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware anchor | Derive the visible sensor/cutout contour, top offset and orientation from the two actual devices. The black body must visually join the target Android cutout without exposing the camera or covering unrelated system information. | External iPhone photo and target Android photo plus window/cutout/inset bounds. Apple does not publish iPhone 18 Pro Max Island dimensions in its current Live Activities table. [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [Android cutouts](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/layout/display-cutout) |
| Shape and color | Store idle, compact, two-item, three-item, expanded and alert contours as measurable geometry, with separate fill, edge/keyline, opacity and content masks. Keep ActivityKit compact/minimal/expanded backgrounds opaque black; publisher color belongs in content and keyline, while Lock Screen background styling is a separate surface. For two items, model the attached and detached minimal contours independently; the detached one can become an oval as its content grows. Do not reuse that rule for all three-item arrangements without capture. Preserve concentric interior margins; derive inner corner radii from the measured outer contour and inset, then optically inspect them. Do not invent a universal 44-point iPhone 18 radius. | Matched reference frames on light and dark backgrounds, including short/long minimal content in R4, all three shapes in R5 and R14. [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [concentric-corner design principle](https://craft.gustavofior.com/nested-border-radius) |
| Publisher spacing | Treat default insets, per-mode `contentMargins` and per-expanded-region margins as distinct inputs. Measure content-to-sensor and content-to-outer-contour gaps rather than deriving all spacing from one radius. A publisher's margin override may change wrapping or visible body size; keep that result observational until R2b proves it. Do not make every Android source use a single universal inset or infer another app's unpublished WidgetKit modifier from a notification. | Controlled R2b fixture with unchanged content and isolated margin changes in each mode, plus paired Android source-specific layout and touch bounds. [Apple custom Live Activity views](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/creating-custom-views-for-live-activities), [expanded-region margins](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/dynamicislandexpandedregion/contentmargins%28_%3A_%3A%29) |
| Content | Leading, trailing, minimal and expanded content keep their identity and relative position as the body changes. Use source-specific content contracts; timer/countdown digits should retain stable widths **if the captured iPhone does**, and icons need optical centering, not just bounding-box centering. The actual font, sizes, baselines, symbols, colors and truncation rules come from native reference frames. Keep reference glyph identity separate from the Android asset and its permitted use under the [typography and icon contract](17-typography-icons.md). | Per-element boxes and baselines in every R-state; timer digit-width, glyph-shape and icon alignment checks plus asset provenance. [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [Apple font overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/fonts) |
| Motion | Model app-to-Island handoff, contour expansion/collapse and content replacement as distinct transition families. Morph the body and move existing content from its previous position; fit easing, overshoot and duration from frame-aligned iPhone footage. Apple caps Live Activity system/custom content animations at two seconds **in addition to** expansion/contraction transitions; that is a content-animation ceiling, not a nominal morph duration. Treat text, symbols, numeric values and multi-element updates as source-specific element transitions rather than one global Island dissolve. Updates must retarget an in-flight transition without a jump. Touch feedback must respond immediately; a release or canceled gesture returns to a documented state. The system does not animate Live Activity updates on reduced-luminance Always-On displays; measure that path separately from unlocked and Reduce Motion states. | R1a source-window/Island trajectories, R6b contour and per-element blur/opacity/scale/position trajectories, interruption trials, tap latency, AOD/reduced-motion capture. Android spring animation is an available mechanism, not evidence of Apple's curve. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [Apple design session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10194/), [Apple animation guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/animating-data-updates-in-widgets-and-live-activities), [Android animation behavior](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/animations/overview) |
| Input and feedback | Align visible and touchable regions; map the iOS 27 Island swipe-down Search/typed-Siri paths separately from Android's notification-shade gesture. On a target build, coordinate its actual search, assistant and shade owners instead of intercepting all top-edge downward swipes. Expose a distinct accessibility action for every active control. Use Android action-based haptics subject to user settings after measuring which iPhone transitions have tactile feedback. | R24/R24a touch map, Search/assistant/shade coexistence, TalkBack focus order and action results, physical haptic check. [WWDC26 keynote](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/101/), [Android custom-view accessibility](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/ui/accessibility/views/custom-views), [Android haptics](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/haptics/haptics-apis) |
| System surfaces | When unlocked, coordinate with status bar and any promoted Live Update chip; on keyguard or dimmed AOD, use a separate Android presentation that reflects the iOS reference's distinct Lock Screen/StandBy behavior. Never show duplicate Android and replica cards for the same event without an intentional rule. | Paired unlocked, shade, lock, AOD, rotation and multi-activity runs with status-bar icons and privacy indicators visible. [Apple Live Activities anatomy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [AOSP SystemUI architecture](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/refs/heads/main/packages/SystemUI/README.md) |

No nominal animation duration, geometry value, color, font substitute or Android density conversion is accepted as an exact target until the iPhone footage and target display justify it. The [interaction contract](13-interaction-contract.md) likewise keeps tap, hold and swipe outcomes source-specific until captured. The visual engine should keep presentation state separate from event adapters so the same measured design can be tested first in a standalone app and later in a device-specific system implementation.

For R6b, log the publisher's actual SwiftUI transition and animation declarations with each reference clip. Compare the same state delta with and without declared timing; fit Android motion to the **observed** curve only if the effect is repeatable. Apple's WidgetKit guide says iOS 16-or-earlier Live Activities ignore animation modifiers, while its current guide describes selectable transitions on later systems; a separate ActivityKit guide still phrases the restriction broadly. This source-level conflict is a reason to measure the iOS 27 build, not to assume a universal system easing. [WidgetKit animation guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/animating-data-updates-in-widgets-and-live-activities), [ActivityKit display guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities).
