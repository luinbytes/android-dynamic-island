# System and publisher experiences

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [iOS reference hub](01-ios-reference.md)

Feature-specific evidence for Island candidates and nearby system surfaces. An iOS status icon alone does not prove an Island card.

- The iOS 27 Lock Screen guide names Apple TV app live sports as a Live Activity, while an older iOS 16 release note explicitly placed Apple TV scores in the Island on iPhone 14 Pro. Apple Sports now has its own Live Activity path. The current iPhone 18 Island placement and whether the two apps publish duplicate or shared game activity remain separate R10r native questions. [iOS 27 Lock Screen guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/access-features-from-the-lock-screen-iphcd5c65ccf/27/ios/27), [iOS 16 release note](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101566), [Apple TV sports contract](../features/50-apple-tv-sports-live-activity.md).
- The same announcement says the redesigned Island continues to accommodate Face ID for unlock, app sign-in and purchase authentication. It does **not** publish the silhouette, animation or timing of any authentication indicator, so measure those paths separately from Live Activities. Authentication success must come from the operating system, not a replica animation. [iPhone 18 Pro announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/).
- Apple's iOS 27 screen-recording guide starts capture after a three-second countdown, places a red recording control in the Island, and lets people stop capture from it; it also says screen recording and screen mirroring cannot run simultaneously. R16 distinguishes countdown, actual recording, stop/save and conflicting mirroring attempts rather than assuming the red Island frame appears at the initial Control Center tap. Apple separately documents an Island flashlight control that changes beam width and length on supported hardware. These are first-party system controls and must be captured separately from third-party ActivityKit layouts. [Screen recording guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/take-a-screen-recording-iph52f6e1987/27/ios/27), [flashlight guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105001).
- Apple's iOS 27 Local Capture guide separately confirms a **recording icon in the Island** while capturing the user's side of a videoconference. Local Capture can be audio and video or audio only; its icon and controls need an R16a native capture. Do not conflate this with screen recording or a generic camera/microphone privacy dot. [Apple Local Capture guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/improve-audio-and-record-video-calls-iph8dc8a5c3c/27/ios/27).
- Apple's Live Activities HIG uses the Timer app's **remaining time** as its example of useful minimal Island content. The iOS 27 Clock guide allows multiple timers to run at once and describes pause, resume, end notifications and Lock Screen actions, but does **not** specify how those timers appear or compete in the iPhone 18 Island. Capture one, two and three concurrent Clock timers separately from the general ActivityKit same-app arbitration test; do not infer an Island slot for every running timer. [Clock timer guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-timers-iph8241d6b2a/27/ios/27), [Live Activities minimal presentation](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).
- AlarmKit separately lets a third-party app publish a countdown Live Activity in the Island, Lock Screen and StandBy and transition through countdown, paused and alerting states. Stop and repeat/snooze are system-handled alert actions; the alert may override Focus and Silent Mode. R10k isolates this documented framework path from Clock timers and generic ActivityKit alerts. Its iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 geometry, priority and exact sound/wake outcome remain to be captured. [AlarmKit sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/alarmkit/scheduling-an-alarm-with-alarmkit), [WWDC25 AlarmKit](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/230/), [AlarmKit lifecycle](../features/33-alarmkit-alarm-lifecycle.md).
- Apple's Push to Talk framework provides a system channel indicator and Talk controls. A WWDC22 example used a blue status-bar pill; a later Apple engineer says a joined channel can show its icon in the status bar **or Dynamic Island**. That does not establish which surface iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 chooses. R10l captures joined, transmitting, receiving, interrupted and left-channel phases as a separate system communication path. [Push to Talk guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/pushtotalk/creating-a-push-to-talk-app/), [Apple engineer answer](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/780822), [channel contract](../features/34-push-to-talk-channel.md).
- Apple's WWDC26 LiveCommunicationKit session explicitly describes third-party incoming, outgoing and group conversations in system UI, including the Dynamic Island. It distinguishes ringing, joining, joined, leaving and left phases, changing audio/video/hold capabilities and group membership. R10m measures the iPhone 18 Pro Max presentation and keeps this path separate from Phone/FaceTime R10c, Push to Talk R10l and SharePlay R28. [Apple live communication session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/226/), [communication contract](../features/45-live-communication.md).
- Apple documents Shazam's real-time music-search progress and result as a Live Activity in the Island or on the Lock Screen on compatible devices. Its guide also describes an Auto Shazam microphone button in the Island that opens Shazam when tapped. R10n distinguishes this **recognition** source from playback and tests manual, Auto, Control Center and Siri entry separately; Apple has not established that all four entry paths have the same Island presentation on iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27. [Apple Shazam recognition](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/118570), [Shazam user guide](https://support.apple.com/en-by/guide/shazam/dev9748744b6/1.0/web/1.0), [recognition contract](../features/46-shazam-recognition.md).
- Apple's current Sports guide documents scheduled, manually followed and automatically followed game Live Activities with scores and plays on the Lock Screen/Watch. The iOS 27 News guide routes Follow Live through Apple Sports and names Home/Lock Screen updates. An iOS 16 note placed **Apple TV** live scores in the Island, but it does not prove Apple Sports' current Island behavior. R10o is therefore a positive-or-negative measurement candidate, including two-game identity and end controls; never treat a current Lock Screen card as proof of a current Island card. [Apple Sports games](https://support.apple.com/en-sg/guide/apple-sports-app/apdc0cb7ad64/web), [iOS 27 My Sports](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/follow-your-favorite-teams-with-my-sports-iph1a30f25eb/27/ios/27), [older Apple TV note](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101566), [sports contract](../features/47-apple-sports-live-game.md).
- Apple's WWDC26 iOS 27 Live Activities session explicitly demonstrates the **MLB app** showing a favorite team's score, inning and key game updates in the Dynamic Island. R10u captures that named third-party publisher separately from Apple Sports/TV; the demonstration establishes the behavior class, not the iPhone 18 Pro Max's exact layout or the Android MLB app's continuous-game feed. [Apple WWDC26 session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/), [MLB contract](../features/53-mlb-live-game.md).
- Apple's upgraded semantic airline boarding pass can create a **Wallet-owned** flight Live Activity using Apple's flight service; an airline can suppress Wallet's version with `associatedStoreIdentifiers` when its own app supplies one. Wallet also supports sharing flight tracking by Messages. This path differs from a third-party tracker, a legacy pass and a gate-change notification. R10p tests current iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 Island presence and phases with a real pass, without assuming the Lock Screen activity defines the Island layout. [Apple Wallet WWDC25](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/202/), [semantic boarding pass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/walletpasses/creating-an-airline-boarding-pass-using-semantic-tags), [flight contract](../features/48-wallet-flight-activity.md).
- The iOS 27 Wallet HIG says the system can start a Live Activity for **certain event tickets**, while pass relevance metadata can separately suggest the pass on the Lock Screen at a date or place. R10q distinguishes those surfaces and tests current Island presence or absence with an eligible test ticket; it does not promote every ticket or Lock Screen suggestion into an Island card. [Apple Wallet HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/wallet/), [pass relevance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/walletpasses/showing-a-pass-on-the-lock-screen), [event contract](../features/49-wallet-event-ticket.md).
- Apple documented a Safari File Download Live Activity with Island progress in iOS 18. That is evidence of a first-party Island state on the older OS, **not** proof of its exact iOS 27/iPhone 18 Pro Max appearance or continued presence. R10i must verify the current build with real downloads, including cancellation, failure and completion. [Apple iOS 18 updates](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121161), [iOS 27 Safari settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/customize-your-safari-settings-iphb3100d149/27/ios/27).
- Apple's satellite roadside-assistance guide describes an off-grid Messages prompt followed by a Dynamic Island tap that opens Connection Assistant. Apple's Messages via satellite guide separately documents Island connection-strength dots, conditional left/right turn arrows and a tap to Connection Assistant during an ongoing link. R27 captures entry; R27a captures real guidance and its changing state. The Satellite Connection Demo under cellular or Wi-Fi coverage remains a separate, guidance-only path; neither source publishes the iPhone 18 contour or timing. [Apple roadside-assistance steps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105098), [Apple Messages via satellite](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120930), [satellite contract](../features/26-satellite-entry.md).
- Apple's SharePlay design guide depicts a short group-activity start message around the Island, with participant imagery badged by the app icon, during a FaceTime shared session. The guide's artwork predates the target iOS 27 build and does not establish a persistent ActivityKit card. R28 must verify the current presentation and its group-session lifecycle on the reference phone. [SharePlay HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/shareplay), [SharePlay start](../features/28-shareplay-start.md).
- Apple's iOS 27 Now Playing framework can surface media playing on a remote device in system Now Playing surfaces, including the Island. Its published example carries a stable remote-session ID, device identities/capabilities, playback snapshot, APNs-driven extension updates and publisher command handlers. These facts define an observable remote source, not the target phone's Island geometry or a guarantee of adoption by every app. Do not assume every iPhone media state represents playback on the phone or that Android can infer a remote player's device and commands without the publisher exposing them. [WWDC26 Now Playing](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/312/), [remote media contract](../features/38-remote-now-playing.md).
- With VoiceOver focused on the Island, the iOS 27 guide says to swipe up through available options and double-tap Explore Screen or Ask About Screen. It also allows other custom Island actions. Those Apple Intelligence options are conditional on device, language/region and settings; record the focus, spoken option order and actual result rather than treating the gesture as a universal Island touch rule or a promised Android feature. [iOS 27 VoiceOver guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph37e6b3844/27/ios/27).
- Apple's camera/microphone status indicators are green for camera (with or without microphone) and orange for microphone alone; the orange dot becomes a square when Differentiate Without Color is enabled. Their location and coexistence with the iPhone 18 Pro Max Island must be captured. An indicator's color does not establish that it is an expandable Island activity. [Apple privacy indicators](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108331), [iOS 27 hardware access guide](https://support.apple.com/en-za/guide/iphone/iph168c4bbd5/27/ios/27).
- The iOS 27 Island guide explicitly names Voice Memo recording, AirDrop connection and Maps directions. Its separate status-icon guide lists phone, FaceTime, screen recording, Personal Hotspot, navigation and charging indicators. A status icon proves an active-session or battery indication, **not** that the item becomes an Island card; keep those questions separate until a native iPhone 18 Pro Max capture resolves them. [iOS 27 Island guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27), [iOS 27 status-icon guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/learn-the-meaning-of-iphone-status-icons-iphef7bb57dc/27/ios/27).
- Apple's iOS 17 developer release notes explicitly mention a Live Voicemail icon in the status bar or Island and a tap opening its transcript. The current iOS 27 guide retains real-time transcription and accept/stop controls, but does not establish that the iPhone 18 Pro Max still uses the same icon location or embeds transcript text in the Island. Capture that handoff separately from an active phone/FaceTime call and call screening (R10s). [iOS 17 release notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/ios-ipados-release-notes/ios-ipados-17-release-notes), [iOS 27 voicemail guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-voicemail-iph3c99490e/27/ios/27), [Live Voicemail contract](../features/51-live-voicemail.md).
- The iOS 27 Phone guide says tapping the green call indicator returns to an ongoing call from another app. Its Hold Assist flow can notify when a live agent is available and offer Answer Call, but the guide does not claim a separate Island Hold Assist card. R10t measures whether the held/agent-return phases change the call indicator or only the Phone screen and notification, then compares the real Android dialer path. [iOS 27 Phone guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/while-on-a-call-iph3c9951d7/27/ios/27), [Hold Assist contract](../features/52-hold-assist-call-handoff.md).
- Apple's AirPods setup guide describes a pairing animation on iPhone, its status-icon guide describes a connected-headphones icon, and its battery guide puts accessory charge in a Batteries widget. None establishes that pairing, reconnection or per-earbud charge is an iPhone 18 Island state. Apple's Controls HIG **does** depict a Silent Mode control's symbol and value in the Island when invoked from the Action button, but does not establish that Settings or Control Center toggles have the same presentation or publish iPhone 18 timing. Capture those paths separately before drawing an Android accessory or ringer pill. [AirPods pairing](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/pair-airpods-with-an-apple-device-dev7c85810f2/27/web/27), [headphones icon](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/learn-the-meaning-of-iphone-status-icons-iphef7bb57dc/27/ios/27), [accessory battery](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/show-the-battery-percentage-iph4747a74a9/27/ios/27), [Apple Controls HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/controls).
- Apple's Controls HIG separately confirms that holding the Action button with an assigned control displays that control's symbol and optional value in the Island. A control may toggle a value or perform a button action without starting a Live Activity. R10j captures this feedback separately from Silent Mode (R10h), a Live Activity start (R22c), and an expanded Live Activity toggle (R18a). The exact target-phone appearance remains unmeasured. [Apple Controls HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/controls), [Action-button feedback contract](../features/32-action-button-control-feedback.md).
- The iOS 27 Voice Memos guide says a recording remains visible in the Island while using another app and says tapping the Island can expand it or return to Voice Memos. Its general Island guide says touch and hold expands an activity. This is a source-level difference in described tap behavior, so test tap and hold separately on the target build rather than treating every compact item as a tap-through link. The Voice Memos guide also says starting audio playback in another app stops its recording; capture that teardown path. [Voice Memos guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-recording-iph4d2a39a3b/27/ios/27), [Island guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27).
- AirDrop can require recipient acceptance or an AirDrop code for a sender outside contacts; transfers between devices on the same Apple Account can auto-accept, and an initiated transfer can continue over the internet after leaving local range. The Island guide confirms an AirDrop connection is visible, but does not specify which of these transfer phases change its shape or controls. Capture them as distinct scenarios and do not infer progress semantics from a generic connection glyph. [iOS 27 AirDrop guide](https://support.apple.com/en-mide/guide/iphone/iphcd8b9f0af/27/ios/27), [Island guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27).
- Apple's WWDC26 keynote says an Island downward swipe can reach **Search or typed Siri**. Apple's later Siri AI release also documents Island-swipe conversation entry, while side-button and voice invocation remain separate paths. Siri AI availability depends on device, language, region and settings. Measure the Search/Siri choice, keyboard focus, Island transition, shade conflict and return with active activities; neither path is an ActivityKit card. [WWDC26 keynote](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/101/), [Apple Siri AI release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/), [Siri AI availability](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-siri-ai-iphv6zwrg8jvfgr/27/ios/27).
