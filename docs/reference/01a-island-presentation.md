# Island presentation and geometry

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [iOS reference hub](01-ios-reference.md)

Apple-published presentation facts and design guidance. Native iPhone 18 Pro Max measurements are still required before pixel parity can be claimed.

- The phone has a 2868 × 1320 pixel, 460 ppi, 120 Hz capable display. These are display facts, **not** Dynamic Island dimensions. [iPhone 18 Pro specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/).
- Apple calls the iPhone 18 Pro Island **smaller** and says it can show **up to three Live Activities at once**. Earlier two-activity layouts and published iPhone 17 widths cannot be used as iPhone 18 Pro Max measurements. [iPhone 18 Pro announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/).
- In iOS 27, compact and minimal Live Activities appear in both portrait and landscape. Apple demonstrates a compact trailing view switching to alternate content when `isDynamicIslandLimitedInWidth` is true; landscape is therefore a content-layout case, not merely a rotation of the portrait pixels. Measure which layouts and thresholds the reference phone actually uses. [WWDC26: Live Activities essentials](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/).
- Apple's `isDynamicIslandLimitedInWidth` environment value applies to **compact-leading, compact-trailing and minimal** views. It does not establish an expanded-view width rule. R8 needs a controlled alternate-content case in each of those three views and a separate native expanded-layout observation. [Apple limited-width API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/isdynamicislandlimitedinwidth).
- Apple describes compact leading and trailing regions, minimal, expanded, and Lock Screen presentations. Touch and hold expands a compact or minimal activity; an alerting update can expand it temporarily. [Live Activities guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities?changes=_5&language=objc), [WWDC26](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/).
- Apple's general Live Activities guidance describes one compact activity or two minimal activities, while the iPhone 18 Pro user guide explicitly allows three at once. Use the iPhone 18 Pro behavior as the target and do not extrapolate its third slot from older two-activity illustrations. [Live Activities anatomy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [iOS 27 user guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27).
- “Up to three” is a visible-presentation limit, not evidence that a fourth accepted activity is ended or that swiping can reach it. Preserve the distinction between active source count and visible slot count, and capture overflow/promotion directly (R5b). [iOS 27 user guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27).
- In Apple's documented two-activity layout, both activities use minimal content: one is attached to the Island and the other is detached. The detached contour can be circular or oval depending on content size. Apple does not give a content-width threshold or explain whether the iPhone 18 three-activity layout reuses those exact rules; capture both questions on the reference phone. [Live Activities anatomy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).
- Apple documents Live Activities in the Island while the iPhone is **unlocked**; the Lock Screen and StandBy use distinct presentations. Treat the Lock Screen as a separate surface, not an Island overlay displayed over the keyguard. [iOS 27 user guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-live-activities-in-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/27/ios/27), [Live Activities anatomy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).
- Apple describes an opaque black Island, content fitted tightly around the sensor, concentric interior spacing, and animated transitions that preserve element position. Its published 44-point corner-radius guidance predates the iPhone 18 redesign; treat it as a design reference, not a verified iPhone 18 measurement. [Live Activities guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities?changes=_5&language=objc), [WWDC23 design session](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10194/).
- Apple's current design resources include an iOS 27 UI kit and a Live Activities template, but the public Live Activities width table located in the HIG lists through iPhone 17 Pro Max and gives no iPhone 18 Pro Max compact/expanded width. Apple's iPhone 18 Pro Max accessory drawing shows **hardware keepout zones**, which are not the visible Island mask or its touch bounds. Neither source supplies a verified iPhone 18 Pro Max Island geometry. [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/), [Live Activities specifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [iPhone 18 Pro Max dimensional drawing](https://developer.apple.com/download/files/accessories/dimensional-drawings/iphone-18-pro-max.pdf).
- An expanded ActivityKit Island can place content in leading, center, trailing, and bottom regions. Apple sizes center content first, then leading/trailing content; region priority and `belowIfTooWide` can move content below the camera when space is constrained. Therefore, an expanded state has content-dependent layouts rather than one universally fixed internal grid. [ActivityKit layout guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities).
- WidgetKit also lets a publisher override the Island's default content margins separately for compact-leading, compact-trailing, minimal and expanded modes, and set margins on an individual expanded region. This changes the space available to content and may change line breaks or expanded height; the API does **not** publish a new universal iPhone 18 outer contour. R2b isolates margin choices from content length and region priority before Android spacing tokens are frozen. [Apple custom Live Activity views](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/creating-custom-views-for-live-activities), [expanded-region margins](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/dynamicislandexpandedregion/contentmargins%28_%3A_%3A%29).
- Apple's Live Activities guidance describes a colored keyline around the Island against dark backgrounds, a maximum two-second duration for Live Activity system/custom animations, and no animation on an Always-On display at reduced luminance. These are general presentation constraints; the iPhone 18 Pro Max line color, contour, timing and dimmed appearance still need native capture. [Live Activities presentation guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).
- Apple's current WidgetKit animation guide says Live Activities on **iOS 16 or earlier** ignore animation modifiers, while its current guidance describes `transition`, `contentTransition`, `animation(_:value:)`, numeric text transitions and custom timing for later systems. A separate ActivityKit guide still uses broader language that modifiers are ignored. Treat modifier support and exact timing on iOS 27 as a controlled R6b observation: compare otherwise identical updates with no modifier, a content transition, a timing modifier and animation disabled. Do not hard-code either documentation statement as the iPhone 18 transition curve. [WidgetKit animation guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/animating-data-updates-in-widgets-and-live-activities), [ActivityKit display guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities).
- In StandBy, a Live Activity's minimal presentation can expand on tap to a Lock Screen presentation scaled 2×, with its custom background extended across the screen. Apple's iOS 27 StandBy guide describes a red tint in low light when Night Mode is enabled. This is a **separate surface and color treatment**, not a red unlocked Island; R11a must capture both modes and the transition. Apple also recommends redacting sensitive Live Activity content on Lock Screen and Always-On; R11 checks the actual publisher/view and user-setting result. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities?changes=_5&language=objc), [iOS 27 StandBy](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-standby-iph878d77632/27/ios/27), [StandBy/privacy contract](../features/36-standby-night-privacy.md).
- ActivityKit says a Lock Screen Live Activity **may truncate** when taller than 160 points. It documents `isActivityFullscreen` as a way for a publisher to detect StandBy, where the system extends `activityBackgroundTint(_:)` across a full-screen activity; WWDC26 shows `showsWidgetContainerBackground` used to choose a different embedded Lock Screen background. Those are publisher-context and Lock Screen/StandBy rules, not universal Island heights or a guaranteed target-phone pixel threshold. Capture short/tall content and both background branches in R11/R11a. [Apple ActivityKit presentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities), [full-screen activity environment](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/isactivityfullscreen), [WWDC26 Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/).
- Apple's Lock Screen Live Activity guidance gives a **14-point standard layout margin** and says the system generates a dismiss button whose color follows the activity's foreground/background; publishers can adjust that auxiliary control with `activitySystemActionForegroundColor(_:)`. On a dimmed Always-On display, the Lock Screen Live Activity is rendered as if in Dark Mode. These are Lock Screen layout/color rules, not measured iPhone 18 Island dimensions. R11/R12b must capture the actual dismiss control, margin, color and action separately from the underlying task. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [Apple custom Live Activity views](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/creating-custom-views-for-live-activities).
- Apple says a publisher cannot customize the opaque black background of compact, minimal or expanded Island presentations, but can give its Lock Screen presentation a custom background. It also directs publishers to vary expanded and Lock Screen height with content. Treat publisher color as foreground/keyline or Lock Screen styling until a native iPhone capture shows otherwise; measure short and long expanded content as separate shapes. [Live Activities color and layout guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).
- Live Activity content changes are a separate animation path from expanding or contracting the Island. Apple documents default blurred text replacement, image/SF Symbol content transitions, and optional numeric and custom scale/opacity/movement transitions; the publisher chooses some of these behaviors. Therefore, capture multiple real publisher update types before encoding a universal Android element transition. The two-second ceiling is not an instruction to make every update last two seconds. [Apple WidgetKit animation guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/animating-data-updates-in-widgets-and-live-activities), [Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities).

## Direct visual evidence from Apple's launch demo

Apple's [8.8-second iPhone 18 Pro Dynamic Island video](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/videos/2026/autoplay/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/apple-iphone-18-pro-dynamic-island/large_2x.mp4) provides a **marketing-rendered** sequence at 1920 × 1080 and about 30 frames per second. It is useful for identifying arrangements and motion intent, but its blurred backdrop, isolated enlarged Island, and camera movement make its pixels unsuitable as native iPhone screen coordinates.

- Around 2–3 seconds, the sequence shows one long pill with content on both sides.
- Around 4–5 seconds, it shows a long navigation pill and a separate small activity on its right.
- Around 6–7 seconds, it shows **three visible shapes**: a small yellow activity on the left, a long navigation pill in the center, and a small orange timer on the right. The side shapes sit close to the central pill but remain detached. This is a concrete visual reference for R5, not proof of how all three-activity combinations are prioritized.
- The video does not show a native touch-and-hold expansion, landscape, Lock Screen behavior, or the phone's true pixel scale. Those remain capture tasks.
