# Samsung Android 16 prototype evidence

This record covers the public-API APK prototype on Samsung SM-S906E (`RZCT81C29ND`), Android API 36, build `S906EXXSDGZB6`, on 2026-09-29. It does not qualify the provisional Pixel 11 Pro XL/Android 17 target or compare against an iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 native capture. Screenshots were inspected locally; raw shade captures are not stored in this repository because they contain unrelated personal notifications.

## Build and installation

- `:app:assembleDebug :app:lintDebug` passed with JDK 17, SDK 36 and one Gradle worker. Lint had no errors; remaining warnings concern intentional durable `SharedPreferences.commit`, tooling-version availability and KTX suggestions.
- The resulting debug APK installed with `adb -s RZCT81C29ND install -r` and launched on the physical phone.

## Observed behavior

| Area | Physical result | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overlay | A bounded pill rendered in portrait and landscape after a temporary display-over-other-apps grant. Tap expanded/collapsed; long press stopped it. Access was revoked after the first pass. | This is an application overlay below Android's system-owned status/lock surfaces. Native contour and motion are unmeasured. |
| Screen sleep | With a timer pill visible, a real `KEYCODE_SLEEP` transition removed the app's `APPLICATION_OVERLAY` window from `dumpsys window`; waking restored it on this device. A display-only `cmd display power-off` did not send the same transition and left the window attached. | The phone returned to the unlocked app on wake, so a secured keyguard and always-on-display privacy transition were not proven. |
| Multiple timers | Two independent countdowns ticked; their state restored after an app force-stop/relaunch. Four sources were retained: three visible and one queued. Selecting the queued fourth source and clearing it promoted the prior queued source. | The arbitration policy is a labeled Android approximation, pending iPhone reference capture. |
| Media listener and transport | A temporary notification-listener grant connected the media feed. A controlled ten-minute audio tone served to Chrome through ADB reverse created one real `com.android.chrome` MediaSession. After explicitly allowing Chrome, the dashboard showed that session and its advertised Play/Pause controls. Tapping Pause changed `dumpsys media_session` from PLAYING to PAUSED, and Play returned it to PLAYING. Revoking listener access cleared the feed. | This qualifies dispatch to one real Chrome session only. It does not prove behavior in Spotify, YouTube Music, Bluetooth or remote playback. No ordinary notification is admitted as an Island activity. |
| Expanded media controls | With the same Chrome session and a temporary overlay grant, the media pill expanded from a 619×146 to an 855×371 px application window. A physical tap in its central action target changed the session from PLAYING to PAUSED; a second tap returned it to PLAYING. The control targets were derived from the current advertised MediaSession actions and the source ID resolved to the exact active token at dispatch. | Secure-window capture prevented visual inspection of the media pill. Chrome's own native media chip was also present; no no-duplicate-chip parity verdict is claimed. |
| Media capture protection | Enabling media without granting listener access caused a fresh Samsung `screencap` of the app dashboard to render black while status/navigation bars remained visible. With the Chrome session selected, `dumpsys window` showed the overlay `fl=1002028`, including `FLAG_SECURE`, and a fresh `screencap` rendered the dashboard and pill black while system bars remained visible. Disabling media made the dashboard capturable again. | This verifies ordinary device screenshots on this build, not screen sharing, Local Capture or locked-screen redaction. |
| Exact timer completion | With temporary `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` and exact-alarm access, `dumpsys alarm` showed an `ELAPSED_WAKEUP` timer alarm with `window=0` and `exactAllowReason=permission`. The app was sent Home and killed with `am kill`, not force-stopped. Android delivered the alarm, restarted the process, and replaced the ongoing notification with a visible `Timer finished` notification. Samsung's shade was visually inspected. | The completion channel is silent and low importance. This does not prove an audible alarm, DND bypass, or delivery after a force-stop. |
| Timer destination | A `dynamicisland://timer-details/<stable-id>` launch displayed the matching finished timer and its clear control at the top of the dashboard. Clearing it removed its persisted record. A later inexact completion notification was tapped in Samsung's shade and opened the matching timer 6 card. | This proves app-owned timer routing only; no third-party notification destination has been accepted. |
| Inexact fallback | After exact-alarm access was revoked, a new timer displayed `inexact timing in use`. `dumpsys alarm` showed `ELAPSED_WAKEUP` with a 45-second delivery window. The app process was killed without force-stop. Android delivered the broadcast about 45 seconds after the requested deadline and showed a finished notification; its tap reached timer 6. | Delivery is delayable; this one run is not a timing guarantee under Doze, battery policy or other devices. |
| Notification controls | A custom `QAControls` five-minute timer appeared in Samsung's shade after Home and `am kill`. The shade's Pause action changed its notification to `QAControls paused` with Resume and Clear. Resume restored the running notification; Pause then Clear removed the notification and left `timers:[]` in app-private storage. | This verifies one app-owned timer control cycle on this device. Stale actions and multi-timer notification interactions have not been exercised. |
| Reboot recovery | A ten-minute app timer was persisted with boot count 16, then the Samsung was rebooted. After boot count advanced to 17, `TimerBootReceiver` appeared as delivered in the broadcast history; app-private state held the same timer ID with a recovered running countdown and boot count 17. `dumpsys alarm` showed a new `ELAPSED_WAKEUP` for that app timer. The timer was then cleared. | This proves conservative reboot rescheduling on this build; powered-off time is intentionally not inferred, and notification delivery was disabled for this pass. |
| Expanded pill controls | Fresh screenshots showed running Pause/Clear, paused Resume/Clear and finished Clear inside the bounded window. An initial tap implementation collapsed instead of pausing; after correcting deferred click handling, physical taps changed the same timer from running to paused to running and Clear left `timers:[]`. The finished Clear control also removed a completed timer. A later APK kept the timer pill rendering and Clear behavior after replacing the single-node accessibility actions with virtual button nodes. Overlay access was revoked afterward. | Samsung TalkBack opened its optional phone-permission dialog; no phone access was granted, and the service was disabled and its dialog stopped. Its focused-button double-tap path remains unverified. |
| Notification action generation | After the generation-bearing APK was installed, one running app timer had persisted `actionGeneration:1`. With the app process killed, Samsung shade Pause changed it to paused/generation 2; Resume restored running/generation 3; a second Pause then Clear removed the timer. The app notification disappeared, and temporary notification access was revoked. | This verifies current actions and durable generation changes. Replaying a stale PendingIntent and migration of an already-finished pre-upgrade notification were reviewed in source, not physically replayed. |
| Missed completion policy | Source review found that a transient `notify()` failure was recorded as a final attempt. The scheduler now retains eligibility after a posting failure and retries on the next reconciliation; unavailable permission or channel at expiry still suppresses the old alert. On the rebuilt APK, a one-minute timer completed through the inexact fallback after `am kill`, the shade showed `Timer finished`, its tap opened timer 15, and Clear removed the persisted timer and active notification. | The transient-failure path is not reproducible through ordinary Samsung Settings and remains source-verified. |
| Direct charging source | With its opt-in switch enabled, a temporary `dumpsys battery set status 2` produced a Charging source in the dashboard and compact pill; status 5 produced Fully charged. While a timer was running, the pill selected the timer and queued the device source. `dumpsys battery reset` restored the original status 4 and removed the source. | Battery status was simulated on this pass; it does not prove charging hardware transitions or all OEM battery-protection states. |
| Direct torch source | The Samsung Quick Settings Torch tile was switched on and the dashboard and compact pill showed Flashlight. The tile was switched off and its checked state returned to false. | This verifies one real hardware toggle; torch callbacks and camera contention may differ on other devices. |
| Dashboard presentation | After moving timer creation ahead of permission and diagnostics details, fresh Samsung screenshots showed the preset and custom timer controls on the initial screen. Further screenshots showed the permission/window cards and the charging/flashlight switches, with engine and window diagnostics at the bottom. | This is a Samsung mobile layout inspection only; large-font, landscape dashboard and screen-reader traversal remain unverified. |

## Access and cleanup

QA uses the phone with brightness at zero and Do Not Disturb (`zen_mode=2`). Temporary access is returned to its prior state after each pass. App-owned disposable timers and notifications are cleared at the end. The app is stopped and its display returned to off. Never treat an installed debug APK or a local screenshot as a release artifact.
