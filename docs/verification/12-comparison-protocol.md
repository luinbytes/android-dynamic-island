# Paired visual and behavioral comparison

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [capture matrix](../reference/02-state-capture.md) · [acceptance](09-implementation-acceptance.md)

This protocol defines how to evaluate every [capture state](../reference/02-state-capture.md) on one iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 build and one selected Android model/build after the [Android device qualification gate](../android/18-device-qualification.md). It is a **proposed measurement method**, not a claim that any current state passes. Preserve the original clips and a record of every alignment, mask and threshold decision so a later reviewer can reproduce a verdict.

## Acquire comparable evidence

1. Record the exact devices, OS builds, published display resolution, **actual file dimensions** and orientation; iPhone/Android appearance, brightness, display zoom, text size, locale and accessibility settings; source app/version and trigger ID. Apple publishes a 1320 × 2868 portrait display grid for iPhone 18 Pro Max, but do not enter that as the saved clip's dimensions without inspecting the file. Capture the same fixture content and state on both devices where a shared fixture exists. Record the Android cutout and status-bar insets. Android `density` is a logical layout scale, while `xdpi`/`ydpi` describe physical pixels per inch; do not turn iPhone points into Android dp by assuming one fixed conversion. [Android DisplayMetrics](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/util/DisplayMetrics), [Apple iPhone 18 Pro specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/specs/).
2. Capture each settled state and transition at least three times, as required by the [capture matrix](../reference/02-state-capture.md). Keep the frame rate and exposure metadata. Use iPhone screen recording for digital content, but use external camera footage for the uncontaminated idle Island and actual physical sensor boundary because recording itself adds an Island indicator. Externally film both phones for display timing, haptics and any capture-excluded system UI.
3. Show a known neutral and a high-contrast background, without changing the system state being measured. Preserve uncropped full-display originals. Declare whether each bounding box is in capture-frame pixels or registered display pixels, and store the transform between them; do not compare raw pixels from different-sized recordings. If filming, keep the camera fixed and perpendicular where possible; register the four display corners or calibrated planar markers to rectify perspective. A homography corrects planar perspective, not lens distortion or different display curvature, so record residual corner/marker error. [OpenCV homography guidance](https://docs.opencv.org/5.0/tutorials/features/homography/homography.html).
4. Align runs to the **actual trigger frame** from the fixture/event log and visible marker, then mark first visible response and settled frame. Do not stretch time to make curves agree. A clip recorded at 60 fps gives 16.7 ms frame intervals and cannot establish 120 Hz presentation timing; report interval uncertainty rather than interpolating missing display frames.

## Measure each layer separately

| Layer | Proposed measurement | Evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware and placement | Display-edge coordinate system; sensor and Island masks; top offset, bounding box, area, centroid, gap to status icons and privacy indicator. Report iPhone pixels, normalized display ratios and physical millimetres as separate values. | A screenshot may omit or alter the sensor edge; external footage needs rectification and physical-scale calibration. A target cutout with a different silhouette may make exact physical parity unsupported. |
| Contour | Extract the black-body silhouette on both backgrounds; compare area/width/height, intersection-over-union and 95th-percentile symmetric contour distance after registered alignment. Keep the actual contour overlays with the record. | An all-black wallpaper can hide an edge; masks and antialiasing decisions require a second background and manual review. |
| Color and keyline | Sample fill, keyline and content away from antialiased edges in the digital frames; report color-space assumptions and per-channel or perceptual differences. Compare external footage only after fixed exposure/white balance and display calibration. | Camera auto-exposure, tone mapping and HDR can create color differences unrelated to the renderer. Do not assert color parity from an uncalibrated video. |
| Content | Measure per-element boxes, baseline, symbol center of visual mass, line breaks, truncation, digit width, source identity and content-to-sensor/contour gaps in compact, minimal and expanded forms. For R2b, record the publisher's declared per-mode and per-region margins alongside the observed pixels. Record reference glyph and Android asset provenance through the [typography/icon contract](../design/17-typography-icons.md). | Similar bounding boxes cannot prove the same font or glyph design; inspect crops, localized variants and text behavior at native scale. The reference asset is not automatically licensed for Android. Publisher margins cannot be reconstructed from a generic third-party Android notification. |
| Motion | Per frame, track contour area, width, height and centroid plus each moving element's position, opacity, blur or scale; report onset, peak/overshoot, settling, canceled-gesture recovery and update-during-transition behavior. Classify content update animation separately from body expansion/contraction. For R6b, log the publisher's declared transition/timing/disable branch and compare identical state deltas; repeat unlocked, with Reduce Motion, and on a reduced-luminance Always-On display. | Compare at observed frame timestamps; separate R1a app handoff, R2 expansion and R6b content replacement. Apple's two-second maximum applies to Live Activity system/custom content animation, not a measured body-morph duration; the reduced-luminance Always-On path should have no system animation. Current Apple guides differ in how broadly they describe ignored animation modifiers, so modifier effect on iOS 27 is an observation, not an assumed rule. A static endpoint match does not prove motion parity. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [WidgetKit animation guide](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/animating-data-updates-in-widgets-and-live-activities) |
| Interaction and system coexistence | Trigger location, touch/hold/swipe outcome, destination, response latency, TalkBack action, haptic/audio result, shade access, status/privacy indicator result, lock/AOD handoff and source teardown. | A visual match cannot pass a broken control, a simulated biometric result or an obscured system indicator. |

The contour and pixel metrics are tools for finding differences, not a substitute for visual review. For mixed hardware, report both **reference-relative** and **target-physical** results. If the Android camera hole prevents the iPhone contour without covering the sensor or system content, record the incompatible geometry directly.

## Set tolerances before tuning Android

For each R-state and measurement, calculate the spread of at least three iPhone repeats, plus capture and registration uncertainty. Freeze a state-specific tolerance and the chosen metric before looking at the tuned Android result. The tolerance must be tight enough to reject an obvious position, contour, font or timing error, and its numerical value and rationale belong in the run record. Do not reuse a tolerance across color, geometry, motion and input, or convert a 60 fps timing interval into sub-frame precision. If repeats disagree because the source state varies, isolate the source variation or leave that layer `unverified`; do not widen the tolerance until everything passes.

An Android implementation may match the captured iPhone **appearance** while differing in **system behavior**. Record `visual`, `motion`, `interaction`, `source fidelity` and `system coexistence` verdicts separately, then assign the row's overall verdict using the weakest required layer. `matched` requires positive paired evidence for every applicable layer and a real Android source/action. `approximate` means a measured difference remains. `unsupported` requires evidence that the selected target route cannot provide the behavior. `unverified` applies whenever a required reference, Android run or measurement is missing. Do not infer `matched` from a source-backed design, a simulator, synthetic events or a passing fixture alone.

## Per-row comparison record

Keep a machine-readable [evidence manifest](18-evidence-manifest.md) beside the original clips, with at least: `state_id`, iPhone/Android build and device, publisher and fixture version, trigger ID, capture provenance and frame rate, alignment transform and residual error, iPhone repeat IDs, frozen tolerance set, measured geometry/color/content/motion/input values, source/action observation, five layer verdicts, overall verdict, reviewer notes and links to evidence. The [state-to-Android map](10-state-to-android-map.md) supplies the expected signal and rendering owner for each row; this record supplies the actual result.
