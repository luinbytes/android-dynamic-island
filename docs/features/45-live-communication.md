# Third-party live communication in the Island

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [R10m capture](../reference/02b-system-activities-capture.md) · [Android event adapters](../android/06-android-events.md)

Apple's WWDC26 LiveCommunicationKit session explicitly presents a third-party real-time conversation from an incoming full-screen Lock Screen UI through multitasking in the Dynamic Island. The publisher reports a stable conversation identifier and changing capabilities through `ConversationManager`; the system sends answer, end, hold, merge and other actions to its delegate. Apple describes `idle` ringing, `joining` connecting, `joined` media flowing, `leaving` teardown and `left` completion. Group membership and **active remote membership** are separate, and two conversations can merge or unmerge when the publisher declares that capability. The session documents integration and lifecycle, not iPhone 18 Pro Max Island pixels or a universal UI for every VoIP app. [Apple WWDC26 live communication](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/226/).

The session shows a **system in-conversation** Mute control: an ordinary tap is Mute, while a long press requests hold only when the publisher declares the pausing capability. Without that capability, the long press does nothing. When hold is requested, the system asks the publisher to pause; the publisher pauses its media streams and reports the changed state, and resume follows the corresponding callback/report path. Capabilities can change during a conversation, so test the same gesture before and after a capability update. The session does not establish that this Mute control sits inside every Dynamic Island presentation; record its actual surface on the target iPhone. [Apple WWDC26 live communication](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/226/).

For an incoming conversation while the publisher is backgrounded, Apple describes PushKit waking the app, followed by a required report to `ConversationManager` before the VoIP push delegate method returns. The session says failure to report in time causes the system to terminate the app. A push receipt is therefore **not** a confirmed conversation or Island entry. Log server send, APNs delivery if observable, publisher wake, reported conversation UUID/state, system acceptance, first ringing surface and first Island frame as separate events. Do not deliberately miss the report deadline in an ordinary acceptance run; a naturally rejected/failed report stays a separate source result. [Apple WWDC26 incoming-conversation sequence](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/226/).

## R10m native capture

Use a controlled LiveCommunicationKit publisher and two or more test accounts. Record incoming audio and video invitations while unlocked and locked, including the PushKit-to-manager report boundary; answer, reject, fail connection, reconnect, change audio route, hold/resume, upgrade audio to video, and end from both the publisher UI and the Island. For the system Mute control, capture tap versus long press with pausing capability absent, present, and changed mid-conversation; log whether any hold action reached the delegate, whether media paused, and where the control appeared. Start a separate outgoing conversation, then run a group with invited versus active participants and, if supported, merge and unmerge two conversations. Log conversation UUID, reported state/capabilities, action invocation/fulfillment/failure, first and last Island frame, compact/expanded content, participant count/names, duration, controls, mic/camera indicators, Lock Screen handoff, tap destination, and coexistence with a cellular call, media and another Live Activity. Use fake contacts and consented test accounts. Keep Phone/FaceTime R10c, Push to Talk R10l and SharePlay R28 distinct. [Apple conversation lifecycle](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/226/).

## Android integration boundary

For a **cooperating Android VoIP publisher**, Core-Telecom is a route to system call coordination: its calling app declares `MANAGE_OWN_CALLS`, registers with Telecom, creates a call with direction/type/capabilities, handles answer/disconnect/active/inactive callbacks, posts its own call notification, and reports real audio endpoints and state. `Notification.CallStyle` exposes incoming answer/decline and ongoing hang-up affordances in Android's notification surfaces. These belong to the calling publisher, not to a standalone Island replica; adding `MANAGE_OWN_CALLS` to the replica does not grant control of another app's calls. Android's Telecom transaction or publisher callback result must confirm a control before the replica reports success. [Android Core-Telecom](https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/telecom/voip-app/telecom), [CallStyle](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications/call-style), [Telecom framework](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/package-summary).

For a **noncooperating VoIP app**, inspect a named publisher/version under the [third-party adapter contract](../android/43-third-party-adapter-contract.md). An enabled listener may receive a `CallStyle` notification and its actual action tokens, but notification text, `CATEGORY_CALL`, or microphone activity alone do not reveal LiveCommunicationKit-like joining, active remote members, merge state or a safe cross-app mute/hold command. Preserve Android's incoming-call/ongoing-call UI, audio focus, privacy indicators and emergency-call precedence. A target-specific SystemUI route may coordinate its own chip and call surface only after the exact Telecom/notification owner is traced; it still cannot invent private publisher phases. [Android CallStyle](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/notifications/call-style), [NotificationListenerService](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService), [Android Telecom](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/package-summary).

The target-build probe uses one cooperating publisher, one inspected third-party publisher and a simultaneous cellular call. Verify source ID through ring → connect → active → hold → resume → end, then group merge/unmerge where genuinely supported. Capture native call notification, SystemUI chip, replica, lock screen, audio route and action owner together. A CallStyle card with only answer/hang-up may support those actions but remains `approximate` or `unsupported` for missing group/hold/video capabilities; visual 1:1 remains `unverified` until paired iPhone/Android footage exists.
