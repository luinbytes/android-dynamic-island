# Shazam recognition Live Activity

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [R10n capture](../reference/02b-system-activities-capture.md) · [Android event adapters](../android/06-android-events.md)

Apple says the Shazam app can show **music-search progress and results** as a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island or on the Lock Screen on compatible iPhones. Shazam also sends a notification when it identifies a song. The search Live Activity is a different source from ongoing music playback (R9/R20), and the notification is a separate surface, so neither should automatically become a second Island session. Apple's Shazam guide documents a microphone button in the Island during Auto Shazam and a tap that opens Shazam to turn it off; it does not establish that every Control Center Music Recognition request, Siri identification, or Android Shazam mode uses this same Live Activity. [Apple Shazam recognition](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/118570), [Shazam user guide](https://support.apple.com/en-by/guide/shazam/dev9748744b6/1.0/web/1.0).

## R10n native capture

On the reference iPhone, update Shazam and record its exact version and Live Activities permission. Start a single manual recognition, leave the app before recognition ends, and capture the searching, match and no-match/offline paths; repeat with Auto Shazam, a second song, another app in front, a locked phone, and one competing activity. Separately start Music Recognition from Control Center and Siri; record **presence or absence** of the Shazam Live Activity for each entry. For every path, record source identity, Island/Lock Screen/notification presence, microphone privacy indicator, compact/minimal/expanded content, tap/hold destination, result lifetime, stop action, source end and competing-activity promotion. Pair external footage with a native recording; the recording itself may add an Island indicator. Apple does not publish iPhone 18 Pro Max contours, motion or per-entry routing, so all remain **measure**. [Apple Shazam recognition](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/118570), [Shazam user guide](https://support.apple.com/en-by/guide/shazam/dev9748744b6/1.0/web/1.0).

## Android publisher boundary

Apple documents the **Android Shazam app** separately: its settings can expose a persistent Tap to Shazam notification, a Pop-up Shazam control and Auto Shazam in single-result or continuous mode. Its guide describes stopping Auto Shazam from an expanded notification. These are candidates to inspect on the selected phone, not proof that it publishes search progress, a match result, a deep link or a stop action to a listener. Capture the installed package/version and actual `StatusBarNotification` key, category, text, actions and `contentIntent` through start, searching, result/no-result and stop. Distinguish a persistent **start** affordance from an active recognition session; only admit the latter when its live publisher fields establish it. Never infer a match from ambient audio, microphone-use state or a generic media session, and do not draw a Stop button unless Shazam publishes a usable action. Keep Android's microphone privacy indicator and Shazam's own notification/pop-up under their native owners. A cooperating recognition publisher can expose explicit task/result IDs and actions, but that proves only its own integration. [Apple Android Shazam settings](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/shazam/devacc2a64ca/1.0/web/1.0), [Shazam user guide](https://support.apple.com/en-by/guide/shazam/dev9748744b6/1.0/web/1.0), [Android notification listener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService).

Record visual, motion, interaction, source/action and system-coexistence verdicts separately. R10n overall stays `unverified` until the iPhone and selected Android runs satisfy the [comparison protocol](../verification/12-comparison-protocol.md).
