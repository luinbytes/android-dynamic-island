# Island swipe to Search or assistant

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [interaction contract](../design/13-interaction-contract.md) · [Android surface ownership](../android/15-android-surface-ownership.md)

Apple's WWDC26 keynote says a downward swipe from the Dynamic Island can lead to **Search or a typed Siri conversation**. Apple's later Siri AI announcement confirms Island swipe entry for a conversation, while its user guide makes Siri AI availability conditional. These sources establish entry paths, not the exact choice screen, gesture threshold, animation, query scope or behavior over active Live Activities on iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27. Keep Search and Siri as distinct R24/R24a outcomes until native capture resolves their relationship. [WWDC26 keynote](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/101/), [Siri AI announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/), [Siri setup guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-and-activate-siri-iph83aad8922/27/ios/27)

The iOS 27 Siri setup guide lists the Island swipe under **"If you have Siri AI"**, and offers a user setting to return to Siri Classic. It says Siri AI begins as a beta, initially in English and outside the EU on iOS. Record exact build, country/region, device and Siri language, Siri AI eligibility and selected Siri mode in each R24/R24a run. A Classic-mode or ineligible-region nonresponse cannot establish a gesture defect; whether Search remains available in those configurations is a separate native observation. [Apple Siri setup and availability](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-and-activate-siri-iph83aad8922/27/ios/27).

Apple's Siri AI guide says a person can swipe down **on a Siri response** to continue revising or ask a follow-up, and the dedicated Siri app can reopen a previous conversation. That is a second gesture after the Island-origin entry and belongs to the assistant response surface; it does not imply the Island keeps receiving the swipe. R24 must distinguish entry, first response, response-surface follow-up, dismissal and later conversation reopening. The Siri app's history is a destination-continuity check, not an Island card or a promise that Android can duplicate Siri's personal context. [Apple Siri AI guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-siri-ai-iphv6zwrg8jvfgr/27/ios/27), [Siri AI announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/).

Apple's iOS 27 Spotlight guide says Spotlight becomes the place to start a Siri conversation when Siri AI is enabled. It documents Search from the Home Screen Search button or a Home/Lock Screen swipe down, and an in-app search swipe from the top when no search field is visible. It does **not** define a chooser for an Island-origin swipe. R24a compares those ordinary entrances with a swipe beginning on the Island; identify the first actual surface and query destination rather than assuming two independent screens. Spotlight results from third-party apps also depend on that app's indexing support, so a missing app result is not an Island input failure. [Apple Spotlight guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph3c511548/27/ios/27).

## iPhone capture

Start from idle, one compact activity, two or three visible activities, and expanded content. Swipe down from the sensor center and either side with measured start coordinates, distance and velocity. Separately launch Search from its Home Screen button, a Home/Lock Screen swipe and an in-app swipe away from the Island, using the same safe query. Record whether the Island gesture first shows a chooser, Spotlight, a text-entry field, Siri conversation, notification shade or no response; then deliberately select Search and typed Siri if both are offered. Enter a nonpersonal query, cancel, submit and return. In an eligible Siri AI run, record the first response, then swipe down on the **response surface** for a safe follow-up; log which window received that gesture, whether the same conversation continues, and whether the original Island activity remains selected after dismissal. Later open the Siri app and determine whether that conversation is available there without recording personal history. Repeat with Siri AI enabled and eligible, Siri Classic selected, Siri disabled, and a genuinely unavailable Siri AI configuration if one can be tested without altering the user's device account. Observe focus, keyboard, active-activity persistence, return selection, status/privacy indicators and app-window transition. Repeat while the app's search result is unavailable and while the notification shade is partially pulled. Source descriptions do not fix the outcome for every configuration.

## Android route ceiling

| Route | Candidate owner | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular APK overlay | The user's installed global-search provider may handle `SearchManager.INTENT_ACTION_GLOBAL_SEARCH`; the chosen assistant may handle its own assist entry. Resolve each on the target before showing a control. [Android SearchManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/SearchManager#INTENT_ACTION_GLOBAL_SEARCH), [assistant role](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/role/RoleManager#ROLE_ASSISTANT) | A public intent is a possible destination, not an Island-owned system gesture or an iOS Search/Siri result. Test no handler, chooser/default change, keyboard focus and return. Do not capture the entire top edge or intercept the shade pull to imitate Apple. |
| Target-specific SystemUI | Inspect the build's actual status-bar/shade, global-search and assistant gesture/window owners. | If a measured Island-origin swipe can be routed without breaking the native shade, film transition, focus, return and accessibility behavior. Do not equate a web-search intent with the target's system search or Siri's conversational functions. [AOSP SystemUI](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/refs/heads/main/packages/SystemUI/README.md) |

Keep three verdicts separate: **gesture and visual**, **Search destination/query**, and **assistant conversation**. A selected Android build may match one without matching the others. Record the user's installed provider and version, assistant role availability, lock state and route. Once a real Android assistant receives entry, its own surface owns follow-up gestures, response state and history; the replica must not intercept them or claim conversation continuity from an Island animation. Compare a named indexed-app query as well as a web query; Android's global-search intent does not establish the same third-party app index or Siri AI actions as iOS Spotlight. When the provider is missing or disabled, show a truthful unavailable path and leave the shade accessible. The overall R24/R24a result follows the [paired comparison protocol](../verification/12-comparison-protocol.md), not the mere existence of an intent constant. [Apple Spotlight indexing](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph3c511548/27/ios/27).
