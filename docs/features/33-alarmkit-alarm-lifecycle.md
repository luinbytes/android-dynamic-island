# AlarmKit countdown and alert lifecycle

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [capture matrix](../reference/02-state-capture.md) · [Android event adapters](../android/06-android-events.md)

Apple's AlarmKit lets a third-party app schedule a one-time or repeating alarm, or a countdown timer. With a countdown presentation, its widget extension supplies a Live Activity for the Dynamic Island, Lock Screen and StandBy. AlarmKit defines scheduled, countdown, paused and alerting states; an alert can stop, repeat/snooze into a post-alert countdown, or invoke a configured secondary action. Apple says the alert can break through Focus and silent mode. This is an **AlarmKit source lifecycle**, separate from iOS Clock's first-party timer and from a generic third-party ActivityKit update. The published sample and WWDC session establish available states and surfaces, not the exact iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 timing, contour or priority. [AlarmKit sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/alarmkit/scheduling-an-alarm-with-alarmkit), [WWDC25 AlarmKit](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/230/), [AlarmKit states](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/alarmkit/alarm/state-swift.enum/countdown).

## R10k native capture

Build a controlled AlarmKit publisher with a unique alarm ID, a ten-minute pre-alert countdown, a short post-alert snooze countdown, custom title and optional secondary action. Capture authorization granted/denied, schedule, countdown, pause, resume, alert, stop, snooze, re-alert and cancel. Repeat a scheduled alarm with no pre-alert countdown as a negative Island control. Keep a second Live Activity active for competition and repeat under Focus, Silent Mode, Lock Screen and StandBy. Record the publisher's `alarmUpdates`, its Live Activity ID and the actual on-screen alert as separate evidence; an absent publisher callback is not proof that the alarm stopped.

For each phase, record first frame, source ID, compact/minimal/expanded content, alert shape and action bounds, wake/sound/haptic, source-app destination, return to the preceding activity, Lock Screen handoff and teardown. Test a pause/resume around the alert boundary, a canceled alarm, and a denied authorization. The current Apple sample says a countdown presentation needs a widget extension; fixture failure to install that extension must not be mistaken for an iOS 27 visual rule. [AlarmKit sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/alarmkit/scheduling-an-alarm-with-alarmkit).

## Android integration boundary

For an **app-owned** Android alarm, keep its alarm ID, wall-clock or elapsed-time deadline, schedule, countdown/paused/alerting state and authoritative stop/snooze result. Use the appropriate `AlarmManager` route for a genuine user-facing alarm; `setAlarmClock()` is the strongly time-critical alarm-clock path. Exact-alarm permissions and OS policy depend on target SDK and selected build. An urgent alarm may use a time-sensitive notification/full-screen intent under Android's permission and visibility rules; the OS owns its lock-screen and heads-up presentation. An Android Live Update is a separate optional ongoing-task surface and its status chip is system-rendered. Do not replace the actual alarm alert with a replica pill or claim Focus/silent bypass from a visual animation. [Android alarm scheduling](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms), [time-sensitive notifications](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/time-sensitive), [Live Updates](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update).

For **another** alarm app, `AlarmManager.getNextAlarmClock()` exposes the next scheduled alarm-clock time, not its countdown, pause, snooze, custom alert actions or current firing state. An enabled notification listener can inspect a specific publisher's posted notification, but the fields and `PendingIntent` actions require a named publisher/version contract. Do not infer an Island countdown merely because the device has a next-alarm icon or turn one generic notification into multiple alarm sessions. A selected SystemUI implementation must preserve the native alarm heads-up/full-screen owner, keyguard and notification shade. [Android AlarmManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/AlarmManager#getNextAlarmClock()), [notification listener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService).

**Parity verdict:** `unverified` until the AlarmKit fixture and actual Android publisher run are captured on the selected phones, including schedule/countdown/alert/snooze/stop/lock behavior. App-owned scheduling and state are direct candidates; cross-app state/actions are publisher-dependent, and the OS alert surface is system-owned.
