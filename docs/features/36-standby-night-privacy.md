# StandBy, Night Mode and sensitive Live Activity content

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [capture matrix](../reference/02-state-capture.md) · [Android surface ownership](../android/15-android-surface-ownership.md)

StandBy is a distinct iPhone surface after the unlocked Dynamic Island withdraws. Apple says a Live Activity starts there in its **minimal** presentation; tapping it transitions to the **Lock Screen** presentation scaled by 2×. A custom Lock Screen background can extend across the screen. StandBy Night Mode gives the Live Activity a red tint in low ambient light. These are presentation rules, not evidence that the unlocked Island itself turns red or becomes full-screen. Exact iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 color, scaling, timing and touch behavior require capture. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities?changes=_5&language=objc), [iOS 27 StandBy guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-standby-iph878d77632/27/ios/27).

## R11/R11a iPhone measurement

Use one controlled activity with a known short and long string, a custom Lock Screen background and separately marked sensitive content. Record unlocked Island → locked/AOD → charging landscape StandBy → minimal activity → tapped full-screen activity → wake/unlock. Hold the publisher content fixed while comparing StandBy Night Mode on/off under controlled ambient light. Log the setting and light level, first red-tinted frame, element/background colors, content bounds, screen-off preference, tap target, scaling and return path. Repeat with a second activity and an alerting update so priority and interruption are observed rather than inferred from a still image. [Apple StandBy guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-standby-iph878d77632/27/ios/27).

For that fixture, log the publisher's `isActivityFullscreen` and `showsWidgetContainerBackground` environment values in each presentation. Apple documents the former as a StandBy detection path and says `activityBackgroundTint(_:)` extends when the activity fills the screen; WWDC26 uses the latter to keep an embedded gradient on Lock Screen while using the tint for an edge-to-edge StandBy background. Compare those two branches with identical content. Separately vary Lock Screen view height around and above Apple's **possible** 160-point truncation guidance and record the actual displayed text, clipping, card bounds and tap targets. Do not apply that Lock Screen guidance to an unlocked expanded Island. [Apple ActivityKit presentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities), [full-screen environment](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/environmentvalues/isactivityfullscreen), [WWDC26 Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/223/).

Apple warns that Live Activities can be viewed on Lock Screen and Always-On and recommends innocuous summaries or redaction for sensitive information. WidgetKit permits sensitive views to be marked with `privacySensitive(_:)`, with placeholder or configured redacted presentation when the user hides that content. For the controlled publisher, compare an innocuous summary, a marked sensitive field and a whole sensitive view under unlocked, locked, AOD, StandBy normal/Night Mode and changed Lock Screen privacy settings. Record which **actual** text, symbols and actions remain visible and whether authentication changes them. Do not assume that an Android notification's privacy metadata is equivalent to the iOS publisher's SwiftUI view redaction. [Apple Live Activities privacy guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities?changes=_5&language=objc), [WidgetKit sensitive content](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/WidgetKit/Creating-a-Widget-Extension).

The standard Lock Screen Live Activity layout margin is 14 points. The system generates a dismiss control to match the activity colors; `activitySystemActionForegroundColor(_:)` can change its auxiliary foreground color. Apple's ActivityKit guidance also says dimmed Always-On Lock Screen activities render as if in Dark Mode, while `isLuminanceReduced` lets the publisher adapt its content. In R11, measure the **rendered** margin, control bounds/color and dimmed content rather than applying 14 points to the unlocked Island or assuming the publisher used the standard margin. In R12b, invoke the real dismiss control and record its effect on the Lock Screen card, source task and later Island state. [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [Apple custom Live Activity views](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/creating-custom-views-for-live-activities).

## Android owner and proof

An APK overlay does not own the keyguard, AOD or StandBy. An Android `DreamService` is a candidate for a user-enabled, interactive charging or docked screensaver, with its own lifecycle and layout; it is not proof that the selected phone launches an iPhone-like StandBy automatically. Android's ordinary dark theme (`UiModeManager` night mode) does not document Apple's ambient-light red tint. An app-owned dream could render a measured red appearance only when its own surface is actually active; a selected SystemUI/OEM route must be inspected before coordinating keyguard, AOD, dream and status-bar content. [Android DreamService](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/dreams/DreamService), [Android night mode API](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/UiModeManager).

For third-party Android tasks, effective lock-screen visibility depends on publisher `VISIBILITY_PUBLIC`/`PRIVATE`/`SECRET`, any `publicVersion`, channel and user/profile policy. Listener access is not a grant to show raw fields in a dream or replica. If that effective policy cannot be established, withhold sensitive third-party fields and controls; test the native notification and replica together while locked, on AOD, in a dream and after profile switch. A SystemUI implementation must use the target build's real privacy decision. [Android notification visibility](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#visibility), [platform privacy gate](../verification/08-platform-proof-gates.md).

An Android notification's dismiss gesture or control may remove its notification and affect the publisher differently from Apple's Live Activity dismissal. The Android route must identify the native lock-card and action owner, record removal reason and source-task continuity, and avoid fabricating an iOS-looking dismiss button that silently cancels work. Its AOD styling and margin must be measured on the selected build as a separate surface from the unlocked Island. [Android notification listener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService), [Android notification visibility](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#visibility).

**Verdict:** R11/R11a remain `unverified` without native and paired target-phone runs. A red dream screenshot alone cannot pass the transition, privacy, AOD, action or system-coexistence layers.
