# Screen capture and sensitive Island content

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [R11c capture](../reference/02c-system-surfaces-capture.md) · [screen-recording owner](../android/25-screen-recording-ownership.md)

Apple documents `isSceneCaptured`/`sceneCaptureState` for an **app scene** during screen recording, mirroring or remote control, and says the app may hide sensitive scene content. That does not establish that ActivityKit's separately rendered Live Activity automatically changes in the Dynamic Island. Apple's Live Activities guidance recommends an innocuous summary or sensitive-content redaction, particularly on Lock Screen and Always-On. The unlocked Island result during capture is therefore a **native measurement**, not a documented automatic redaction rule. [Apple capture-state guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/protecting-sensitive-content-when-screen-sharing), [Apple Live Activities HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities), [WidgetKit privacy guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/WidgetKit/Creating-a-Widget-Extension).

For R11c, use a controlled publisher with one innocuous and one marked-sensitive field in its compact, minimal, expanded and Lock Screen presentations. First capture an uncontaminated baseline externally. Then separately start whole-screen recording, app-only sharing if offered, AirPlay mirroring and remote-control sharing on the iPhone 18 Pro Max/iOS 27 build. Compare what the person sees physically, what a recipient/capture sees, the publisher app scene's capture-state callback, the Island's field placeholders, recording/mirroring indicator, alert expansion, touch results and state after capture ends. Repeat locked/unlocked, with another activity and with publisher app foreground/background. A native screen recording may itself place a red indicator in the Island and cannot establish an uncontaminated baseline; use synchronized external footage and an allowed receiving-side capture. Do not record personal data. [Apple screen recording](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/take-a-screen-recording-iph52f6e1987/27/ios/27), [Apple scene capture](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/protecting-sensitive-content-when-screen-sharing).

On Android, `WindowManager.addScreenRecordingCallback()` on API 35+ reports visibility of **this app's activities** in a recording. It does not report every other app's projection or guarantee a callback for a standalone replica overlay. `FLAG_SECURE` protects the window to which it is applied from screenshots and non-secure displays; Android also documents `View.setContentSensitivity()` as marking a window secure during media projection. These are protection mechanisms for the replica's own window/content, not a feed for a third-party publisher's sensitivity or a way to change SystemUI's native chip. An app-only share can exclude the replica even when whole-screen recording includes it. Test both modes and the selected build's actual outcome. [Android 15 recording detection](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/features#screen-recording-detection), [secure window flag](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowManager.LayoutParams#FLAG_SECURE), [content sensitivity](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/View#setContentSensitivity(int)).

Android 15 also protects **native notification content during screen sharing**: a publisher's `publicVersion` can replace its private notification for remote viewers, while an absent public version yields redaction. Google's documented default system recorder exception means a local saved recording can differ from the remote receiving-side image. Keep native shade/chip privacy, listener payload, replica overlay pixels and remote output as separate evidence; a replica with listener access must not re-expose a field that SystemUI hides from the recipient. Test both publisher public-version branches and the actual target build, then assign a privacy verdict independently from contour matching. [Android 15 privacy changes](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-all#security).

**Content Capture is a separate system ingestion path from a screenshot or media projection.** For apps targeting Android 17/API 37+, Android says the deprecated `ContentCaptureManager.setContentCaptureEnabled(false)` no longer disables Content Capture; it directs apps that need to restrict their own window content to `FLAG_SECURE`. A replica targeting API 37 must therefore test its own actual overlay window's Content Capture treatment on the selected build, as well as saved screenshots, whole-screen recording and app-only sharing. Do not use an app Activity's Content Capture setting as proof that another app's content or SystemUI's native chip is protected. Record the chosen protection policy and any intentional difference between what is visible physically and what a recipient or system capture receives. [Android 17 target behavior](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/behavior-changes-17#deprecate-setcontentcaptureenabled), [secure window flag](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowManager.LayoutParams#FLAG_SECURE).

For a named Android publisher, retain its effective lock/profile visibility and explicit sensitive-field contract; a notification listener grant does not authorize replaying private text into an unrestricted overlay or shared screen. Where capture state cannot be observed reliably, prefer the publisher's nonsensitive summary or withhold the field; record the resulting functional/visual difference. If the replica uses `FLAG_SECURE` or content sensitivity, externally film the visible Island and separately inspect the saved recording/recipient display, then verify that tap targets and native recording controls still work. Score unlocked visual parity, captured-output privacy and lock-screen privacy independently. All remain `unverified` without paired native results. [Android notification visibility](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#visibility), [Android recording callback](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowManager#addScreenRecordingCallback(java.util.concurrent.Executor,java.util.function.Consumer)).
