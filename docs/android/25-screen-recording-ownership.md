# Screen recording: detection and control ownership

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [R16 capture](../reference/02-state-capture.md) · [Android permissions](07-android-permissions.md)

## Separate the three signals

| Signal | Public Android contract | What the Island may infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This app owns a recording | An app that starts a `MediaProjection` session knows its own session lifecycle and receives projection callbacks. | Show and stop **that** recording only; follow the consent, foreground-service and stop rules for the chosen Android release. [MediaProjectionManager](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/projection/MediaProjectionManager), [MediaProjection](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/projection/MediaProjection) |
| This app's activity is visible in a recording | Android 15/API 35 adds `WindowManager.addScreenRecordingCallback()` with the normal manifest permission `DETECT_SCREEN_RECORDING`. It returns the initial visibility state and calls back when an activity owned by the registering process's UID becomes visible or invisible in a screen recording. | This is a **privacy signal for the app's own activities**, not a device-wide recording state. A replica overlay over another app must not turn this into a global red recording pill or offer a stop button. [Android 15 feature](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/features#screen-recording-detection), [WindowManager callback](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/view/WindowManager#addScreenRecordingCallback(java.util.concurrent.Executor,java.util.function.Consumer)), [permission](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/Manifest.permission#DETECT_SCREEN_RECORDING) |
| System or another app owns a recording | Android 15 QPR1 documents a native media-projection status-bar chip and stop affordance. A notification or a visible chip can be inspected on the selected build, but the app-owned callback does not expose the owner, session, microphone state, saved result or stop authority. | Keep the native chip and its real stop path. A third-party card requires an inspected publisher notification/action or an explicit integration; a selected SystemUI implementation may coordinate with its own projection owner after a build-specific audit. [Android 15 QPR1 behavior](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-all#media-projection-status-bar-chip), [notification listener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService) |

`DETECT_SCREEN_RECORDING` is install-time normal access, so include it **only** if the app actually uses the callback for privacy behavior in its own activities. It does not improve R16 global recording coverage. Do not treat `DETECT_SCREEN_CAPTURE` (screenshot detection) as equivalent. [Permission](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/Manifest.permission#DETECT_SCREEN_RECORDING), [screenshot API](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/features/screenshot-detection).

## R16 proof on the selected Android build

Record the Android release and QPR/build, system recording owner, projection chip location, countdown or permission prompt, confirmed start, stop path, microphone toggle, saved/error result and lock behavior. Run an app-owned projection, SystemUI recording, and a different app's projection separately; attempt concurrent mirroring as its own outcome. For each, note whether the replica has a real source signal and an authorized action, whether the native chip is duplicated or obscured, and what survives rotation, app switching, lock and permission revocation. Where an app-owned activity exists, check the callback's initial state and visibility transitions; repeat with only the replica overlay above another app to test the callback boundary. Pair user tap, countdown, confirmed start, stop and result timestamps with external iPhone R16 footage before assigning any visual or functional parity verdict.
