# Android system-surface ownership

[Specification index](../../REFERENCE_SPEC.md) · [Android architecture](05-android-architecture.md) · [state machine](../design/14-state-machine.md)

This is an Android integration contract for a **selected device and OS build**, not a claim that an ordinary APK can control SystemUI. A source activity, notification, native chip, replica drawing and action destination are distinct objects. The build must identify their real owners before placing a Dynamic Island renderer near the camera.

## Owner and capability matrix

| Surface or action | Current Android owner | APK prototype responsibility | Target-specific SystemUI proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Status-bar window, clock, connectivity and cutout spacing | SystemUI and display/window policy | Measure insets and actual occlusion; keep its overlay bounded and leave native icons usable. | Trace the selected build's status-bar window, content insets and screen decorations; reserve real space for the Island in every rotation. [AOSP SystemUI](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/refs/heads/main/packages/SystemUI/README.md) |
| Promoted Live Update chip | SystemUI, after notification-manager promotion | Observe the system-set `FLAG_PROMOTED_ONGOING`; do not claim the overlay can replace or suppress the chip. | Trace chip presentation, touch and accessibility owners; choose and verify one behavior for a matched source instead of accidentally showing competing controls. [Live Update API](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update), [promoted flag](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#FLAG_PROMOTED_ONGOING) |
| Notification shade and promoted card | SystemUI notification pipeline | Keep shade pull and notification tap intact; do not capture the entire top edge. | Reuse the native notification's identity, ranking, effective visibility and dismissal policy; verify shade gestures from every Island state. [Notification listener](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService) |
| Lock screen and Always-On | Keyguard/SystemUI notification policy | Withdraw or withhold private third-party content according to effective device state; an APK has no authority to own the lock layout. | Apply native per-user/profile redaction and separate lock/AOD presentation, then verify unlock handoff. [Android notification visibility](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#visibility) |
| Privacy indicators and usage details | SystemUI and PermissionController | Avoid covering indicators or their tap target. | Preserve platform camera/mic/location indicator source and details route while animating the Island. [AOSP privacy indicators](https://source.android.com/docs/core/permissions/privacy-indicators) |
| Quick Settings and assistant entry | SystemUI plus the chosen assistant or publisher `TileService` | Observe a publisher's resulting source; leave native panel and assistant gestures available. | Route touch to the actual shade/assistant/publisher owner and prove that locked/unavailable paths remain usable. [Quick Settings tiles](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/quicksettings-tiles), [assistant role](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/role/RoleManager#ROLE_ASSISTANT) |
| Authentication and screen recording controls | Keyguard/biometric and SystemUI recording owners | Yield to secure UI and real recording controls. | Trace the target owners and retain their actual success/failure/stop results; never let a drawn glyph authorize or stop a different source. [BiometricPrompt](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/hardware/biometrics/BiometricPrompt) |

For R21 on an Android 17 handheld, include a **foreground non-system app using precise location** as a separate privacy-indicator fixture. Android 17's compatibility definition requires a visible indicator for that condition; it does not establish the same indicator for approximate-only or background access. Keep the native location indicator and its details route clear of the Island, and record its actual location, timing and coexistence with camera/microphone indicators on the chosen build. [Android 17 handheld compatibility definition](https://source.android.com/docs/compatibility/17/android-17-cdd), [AOSP privacy indicators](https://source.android.com/docs/core/permissions/privacy-indicators).

## Live Update eligibility is not chip visibility

For an **app-owned** task, record a sequence of independent facts: notification permission, channel importance, allowed style, ongoing flag, title, absence of custom content/group-summary/colorized treatment, manifest `POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS`, request for promotion, `canPostPromotedNotifications()`, actual `FLAG_PROMOTED_ONGOING`, and actual on-screen chip/card. Android requires a channel above `IMPORTANCE_MIN`; OEMs can add criteria. The promoted flag is system-set and readable by the publisher or listener. `hasPromotableCharacteristics()` is a format check, not a guarantee that user or OEM policy will promote it. On builds with the promotion API, `POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS` is in addition to the app's `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime grant for notification posting; the promotion permission is `normal|appops` in the current API reference. Check the selected OS/API extension before enabling this adapter. [Android Live Update requirements](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update), [permission contract](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/Manifest.permission#POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS).

The native chip is a limited system component: it always includes an icon, may include short text or a countdown, and has a 96 dp maximum width. It is not an arbitrary Dynamic Island canvas. Use the selected build to decide whether the chip and replica can coexist without duplicated affordances; if the chosen SystemUI branch replaces the chip for one source, record its fallback when the Island feature is disabled or crashes. Do not publish a Live Update just to advertise the replica; Android's usage rules reserve it for a genuinely ongoing, user-initiated, time-sensitive task. [Android Live Update chip and usage rules](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update).

Promotion can be a **phase of one task**, rather than the task's start. Android's guidance uses a boarding pass that remains a normal notification until the user's journey becomes imminent, then becomes a Live Update. Model the source task, notification key and promotion state independently so an update or demotion does not create a second Island source or imply task completion. For a user-requested background monitoring task such as a sports game, the publisher should include an Unpin action in its notification; only expose that action in the replica when the real publisher supplies an executable token. Keep the chip and card's timestamp or duration format consistent when the publisher controls both, and record any target-build difference instead of rewriting another app's fields. [Android Live Update usage](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update), [Android Live Update design](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/home-screen/live-updates).

For a promoted notification, `setShortCriticalText` (API 36; suggested maximum seven characters) is the chip's highest-priority content when supplied. An empty string explicitly requests an icon-only chip; `null` permits fallback. On API 37+, a `MetricStyle` critical metric is the next candidate; a positive chronometer or positive time remaining from `when` is the last candidate under the documented builder rules. The system can still omit content if it cannot fit or in certain states. This is a **native chip precedence rule**, not a rule for choosing the replica's compact text. Record the publisher's actual field values before interpreting a short, blank or changing chip. [Android notification builder](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.Builder#setShortCriticalText(java.lang.String)).

### Android 17 publisher styling and chip content

Android 17 (API 37) adds `Notification.MetricStyle` for publisher-owned metrics, including an optional critical metric that may be used on a compact surface such as the status chip. It also adds semantic styles for eligible notification text, metrics and `ProgressStyle` points/segments. Those styles express informational, safe, caution or danger meaning; they do not give an app control of chip geometry, Z-order, animation or another publisher's appearance. A `ProgressStyle` point/segment's explicit color overrides its semantic style. Treat this as a richer **publisher data contract** on API 37+, not a new replica rendering route. Check the actual selected build and promoted state before claiming that a metric or semantic color appears in its chip. [Android 17 features](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/features), [MetricStyle critical metric](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.MetricStyle#getCriticalMetric()), [ProgressStyle segment](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.ProgressStyle.Segment#setSemanticStyle(int)).

For an app-owned timer, workout or journey fixture on API 37+, record the publisher's style, each metric's identity/value and the critical-metric index; then compare the posted notification, promoted chip and listener-visible payload. For a third-party publisher, use only fields actually present in the delivered notification. Neither a semantic danger style nor a critical metric proves iPhone Live Activity priority or a matching Island color. If the selected phone is on API 36 or lower, omit this branch and keep the existing `ProgressStyle`/short-text tests. [MetricStyle API level](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.MetricStyle), [Android Live Update requirements](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update).

API 37 also lets a publisher hint at each notification action's relative emphasis (`AUTO`, `PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`) and preferred presentation (`AUTO`, `TEXT_ONLY`, `ICON_AND_TEXT`, `ICON_ONLY`). These hints may influence a promoted ongoing notification, but the system is not bound to honor them. `ICON_ONLY` still requires a non-null action title, and a hint never creates an action or grants permission to invoke another publisher's `PendingIntent`. Keep the action's semantic meaning, authentication requirement, real `PendingIntent` and source ownership separate from its visual hint. For third-party activities, read the delivered action fields; do not synthesize an iOS-looking control from an absent or disabled Android action. [Action builder](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.Action.Builder#setStyleHint(int)), [action constants and title requirement](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification.Action#STYLE_ICON_ONLY).

## Reconciliation for a single source

Use `(Android user/profile, publisher package, notification key, publisher session ID when supplied)` as a candidate identity. The notification key is authoritative for one posted notification; a publisher session ID or documented media-session relationship is needed before merging it with a media record. Package alone must not collapse two timers or deliveries. Capture each of these independently:

1. **Post/update/ranking:** listener receives `onNotificationPosted`; refresh the exact fields and supported actions. Process `onNotificationRankingUpdate(RankingMap)` independently against active keys, because rankings are snapshots and may change without new publisher content. On connection, resnapshot active notifications and current ranking before showing them as current. A ranking callback alone does not prove that a chip appeared or disappeared. [NotificationListenerService](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService), [Ranking snapshots](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService.Ranking).
2. **Promotion/demotion:** inspect `FLAG_PROMOTED_ONGOING` on a fresh notification and film the actual chip. A missing flag can mean several things; do not label it a user demotion without the corresponding device interaction and trace. Keep the replica's source truth separate from native chip visibility. [Promoted flag](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/app/Notification#FLAG_PROMOTED_ONGOING).
3. **Dismissal/removal:** record `onNotificationRemoved(..., reason)` when delivered; match by the notification key because the removed object can omit heavy fields. Invalidate its actions at removal, then independently verify whether the publisher task ended. A publisher-owned `setDeleteIntent` can observe dismissal of its own Live Update. Do not automatically repost a user-dismissed app-owned Live Update. [Listener removal reasons](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/notification/NotificationListenerService), [Live Update dismissal guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/live-update).
4. **End or access loss:** the real publisher ends its task, removes/changes the notification, or the listener loses access. Invalidate its exposed controls immediately and reconcile media or another documented source independently; an exit animation must not retain a live action.

For a normal APK, the safest **unverified** coexistence policy is to leave the native chip in place and withhold any replica tap target that overlaps it. That may visibly differ from iOS and must be scored `approximate` if measured. For a SystemUI build, select the chip replacement/coexistence policy only after inspecting the exact target implementation and the R4/R5/R5b source arbitration; neither public notification APIs nor an overlay grant authorize suppression of another app's chip.

## Target-build acceptance sequence

- Run one eligible publisher notification and one ineligible variant for **each** documented criterion: missing title, `IMPORTANCE_MIN`, custom content, group summary, colorized, non-ongoing, disallowed style, no promotion request, and user-disabled promotion. Capture the actual flag and on-screen surface; use real ongoing tasks only for promoted cases.
- Run two promoted publishers plus a media session, then a fourth active source. Capture chip choice, Island slots, status icons, privacy dot, shade card order, tap destination and accessibility focus before and after a source end. No Android ordering should be reported as Apple's until paired R5/R5b footage proves it.
- On API 37+, add a publisher-owned `MetricStyle` case with two metrics and a changed critical metric, plus promoted `ProgressStyle` semantic-style cases. Capture the native chip/card and listener record after each update; classify missing/ignored styles as target-build observations, not as replica renderer failures.
- On API 36+, vary `setShortCriticalText` through a short value, `""` and `null` on one real promoted task while holding its source state fixed. Then vary `when`/chronometer and, on API 37+, the critical metric beneath it. Capture actual chip content and available width after each update, including cases where text cannot fit; verify the replica still shows the source's intended compact content and does not duplicate a hidden or icon-only chip label.
- On API 37+, post an eligible publisher fixture with two real actions and vary their emphasis and icon/text style hints. Record delivered action fields, promoted chip/card rendering, action accessibility labels, actual tap destination and locked authentication behavior. Repeat with an absent or null-intent action; never infer that a styled button is executable merely because it renders.
- User-demote and user-dismiss a Live Update in separate trials, then update and end the publisher. Record callback reasons, flag changes, chip/card visibility, replica behavior and any publisher `deleteIntent`; do not infer one user's intent from a single absent flag.
- Keep one publisher task and notification key through ordinary → eligible/promoted → user-demoted → ordinary/ended phases. Log source state, notification fields, promotion request/flag, chip/card timestamps, actual shade/lock presentation and replica source ID at each phase. Repeat with an explicitly user-requested background monitoring task whose notification has a real Unpin action; prove the action changes publisher state before any replica control claims success.
- Repeat unlocked, shade-open, locked, AOD, landscape, app fullscreen, SystemUI restart and user/profile switch. Record the first frame after each transition and verify that no duplicate renderer or private content survives the switch.

The acceptance record stores the **source truth**, **native Android surface**, **replica surface**, **input/action owner** and **paired iPhone result** separately. A correct listener callback or eligible Live Update is only evidence for part of that record; it is not a 1:1 verdict. Use the [comparison protocol](../verification/12-comparison-protocol.md) for the final per-state result.
